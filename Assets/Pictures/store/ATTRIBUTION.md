# Store product photos

Photos of the product types sold in the Fog of the Dragon store, taken from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses.
They show the kind of item (a mug, a hoodie, a dice tray); the Fog of the Dragon print design for each product is drawn by `JS/Dashboard/marketplace/store-art.js`.
Photos may be cropped or scaled in the page. No photo shows a third-party logo or official D&D artwork.
