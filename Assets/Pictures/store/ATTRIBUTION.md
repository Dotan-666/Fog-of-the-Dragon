# Store product photos

Photos of the product types sold in the Fog of the Dragon store, sourced from Unsplash, Pexels, Wikimedia Commons, and other free-license sources.
They show the kind of item (a mug, a hoodie, a dice set); the Fog of the Dragon print design for each product is drawn by `JS/Dashboard/marketplace/store-art.js`.
Photos may be cropped or scaled in the page. No photo shows a third-party logo or official D&D artwork.
