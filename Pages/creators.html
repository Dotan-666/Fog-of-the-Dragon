Free Community Homebrew

You don't need to be a verified creator to share your work. Any FOD user can publish free homebrew to the Community Marketplace with a declaration that the content is their own. The Creator Program is for selling paid content.

Creator Guidelines

Follow these to keep the marketplace fair, legal and high-quality for everyone.

Original Content Only

Everything you list must be your own work. No copied text, art or mechanics from published books.

SRD & OGL Content

You may use content from the Systems Reference Document under the OGL or CC-BY-4.0, with proper attribution.

No Product Identity

Do not use trademarked names, settings or characters from any publisher without explicit license.

Quality Standards

Listings are reviewed for clarity, completeness and formatting before publication.
