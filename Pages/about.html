A companion for the table, not a replacement for it.

Fog of the Dragon is a campaign companion for tabletop role-playing games. It keeps the notes, the schedule, the characters, the initiative order and the music, so the people at the table can keep their eyes on each other.

What we believe

Three ideas decide what we build, and what we leave out.

  • The table comes first

    Fog of the Dragon is not a Virtual Tabletop. Dice, miniatures, maps and the faces across the table stay exactly where they are; the app sits beside them.

  • Bookkeeping belongs to the machine

    Recaps, scheduling, turn order and rule look-ups are the parts nobody misses doing by hand. Technology should handle the memory and the math, and leave the storytelling to people.

  • Players join for free

    One Dungeon Master’s plan covers the whole campaign. Nobody at the table has to pay to take their seat.

What’s inside

One place for the whole campaign, from the first character to the last recap.

Campaigns
A shared home for the party: members, meetings, a feed, and voice and video for remote tables.
Characters
A guided builder for new players and a quick one for veterans, with a guide to what each character can do.
Combat
Initiative, hit points, conditions and attacks resolved against the real numbers, with the Dungeon Master in control.
Music & sound
Licensed tracks, ambience and effects, with playlists and cues for each scene.
Adventures
Chapters, scenes, encounters, maps and private DM notes, ready to run and to share piece by piece.
Logbook
Sessions, recaps and memories, so “what happened last time?” always has an answer.
Foggy
A writing assistant for the Dungeon Master. It drafts and suggests; you decide what is kept.
