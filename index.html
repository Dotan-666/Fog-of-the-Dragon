THE TABLETOP DILEMMA

Great stories get lost in the friction.

Campaign drag rarely happens during the narrative. It happens in the two weeks between games — in forgotten notes, rule disputes, and scheduling paralysis.

PLAYER DILEMMA

"What happened last session?"

Scattered notebooks, forgotten NPC names, and lost quest hooks force your party to waste the first 30 minutes catching up.

GROUP DILEMMA

"When are we playing next?"

Endless group chat pinging and scheduling delays break your campaign's momentum and stall great stories indefinitely.

COMBAT DILEMMA

"What can my character do?"

Flipping through rulebook PDFs during turn orders kills combat rhythm while the DM manually tracks creature stats and spells.

THE FOG OF THE DRAGON WAY

Fog of the Dragon handles campaign management quietly in the background — so your table stays focused on rolling dice and telling stories.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Your physical table is where the game lives.

Fog of the Dragon is not a Virtual Tabletop (VTT). We believe the true magic of tabletop RPGs happens through real human connection, physical dice rolls, and theater of the mind.

✕

We Do Not Replace

  • ✕ Physical dice and the thrill of natural 20s
  • ✕ Miniatures, battlemaps, and physical terrain
  • ✕ Face-to-face eye contact and table atmosphere
  • ✕ Pure theater of the mind storytelling
✓

We Enhance

  • ✓ Between-session recaps & memory tracking
  • ✓ Frictionless party scheduling & session links
  • ✓ Instant rule clarifications & ability breakdowns
  • ✓ Immersive audio soundscapes & combat tracking

"Technology should handle the bookkeeping, memory, and math—leaving players free to do what humans do best: roleplay, improvise, and build legendary stories together."
